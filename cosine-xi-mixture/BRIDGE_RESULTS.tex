\section*{B1: finite-window critical-line transfer}
Fix a finite ordinate window $[T,2T]$ and retained simple critical-line zeros
\[
\Gamma_T=\{\gamma_1<\cdots<\gamma_s\},\qquad
\Xi(\gamma_j)=0,\quad \Xi'(\gamma_j)\ne0.
\]
For any prescribed $\eta_T>0$ and lower threshold $\Lambda_*(T)$ there is
$\Lambda_{C1}(T)\ge\Lambda_*(T)$ such that every
$\lambda\ge\Lambda_{C1}(T)$, with
\[
t=\frac{e^\lambda}{1+e^\lambda},
\]
gives for each $j$ a unique simple real zero $r_j(t)$ of
$H_t=(1-t)\cos+t\Xi$ satisfying
\[
|r_j(t)-\gamma_j|<\eta_T.
\]
For disjoint localization neighborhoods the order is preserved.

\section*{B2: local multiplicity splitting}
Let $f$ have a real zero $\gamma$ of multiplicity $m\ge1$, and let $g(\gamma)\ne0$.
For all sufficiently small real $\varepsilon\ne0$, each perturbation
$f+\varepsilon g$ and $f-\varepsilon g$ has exactly $m$ simple roots in a sufficiently
small disk around $\gamma$. Across the two perturbations together, exactly two roots are real and
\[
\boxed{2m-2}
\]
are nonreal.

In the $\Xi$ application one may choose a cosine direction nonvanishing at the retained zero;
the campaign uses the pair $\cos z$ and $\cos(\sqrt2\,z)$ to avoid simultaneous vanishing.

\section*{B3: finite-window defect identities}
Let
\[
E_{\mathbb R}=\sum_{\gamma\ \mathrm{real}}(m_\gamma-1)
\]
over the retained real zeros in pairwise disjoint localization disks, and let
$D_{\mathbb R}$ count the paired local nonreal roots generated from them. Then
\[
\boxed{D_{\mathbb R}=2E_{\mathbb R}}.
\]
If $N_{\rm off}$ is the total multiplicity of retained nonreal $\Xi$-zeros in the same
finite localization scheme, then the full paired local defect obeys
\[
\boxed{D_{\rm all}=2N_{\rm off}+2E_{\mathbb R}}.
\]

These are finite/local identities. No endpoint-scale $o(N)$ global defect budget is claimed.
